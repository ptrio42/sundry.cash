What it notices

Most trackers answer the questions you ask. Sundry opens with the ones you did not — these four sentences are its own, printed by the demo on 12 August 2026:

  • Gym membership looks like it stopped — nothing since 3 Apr 2026, after 1192,00 zł at about 146,31 zł a month.
  • Entertainment is up 146% over the last 30 days — 1002,27 zł, against 407,99 zł before.
  • Żabka adds up — 1734,88 zł across 108 purchases in the last 366 days, about 16,06 zł each.
  • Weekends cost more — about 225,55 zł a day over the last 366 days, against 81,34 zł on weekdays.

The demo re-seeds every night against the day it runs, so the figures waiting for you will be different ones.

How it works

1. Add what you spent

Type it, photograph the receipt, or import a spreadsheet.

2. The first screen does the noticing

What changed, what stopped, where it went — no filters to set, no dashboard to build.

3. It is on your phone

The same app, from the browser, on your home screen.

On your phone

Open Sundry in your phone's browser and add it to the home screen. It opens full screen from there, with no browser bars, and the camera is one tap away for a receipt.

On an iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, Chrome offers Install.

Nothing to download, and it updates itself.

See it with your own eyes

demo.sundry.cash is a real instance with about eighteen months of made-up spending in it. No signup, no password — the link opens the app, and you can add, edit and delete like it is yours.

It is wiped and re-seeded every night, so anything you add there is gone by morning.

Receipt scanning is switched off on the demo.

Pricing

€5 a month, or €49 a year. VAT included, one price everywhere.

The first 30 days are free. A card is required to start, and nothing is charged if you cancel inside the 30 days.

You get your own instance at your own address, with everything the product does. Support is by email, from the person who wrote it.

Start the 30 days

Sundry is a subscription. It runs until you cancel it, you can cancel at any time, and cancelling stops the next payment. The terms carry the 14-day right of withdrawal, the model withdrawal form, and who you are buying from.

What it does not do

Better you know now than after signing up.

No bank sync.
You add expenses yourself — typed, from a receipt, or from a spreadsheet.
One person per account.
There is no shared household ledger.
Expenses only.
No income, no investments.

Privacy

  • It never asks for your bank credentials. There is nowhere to enter them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers. This page loads nothing from anyone else — open the network panel and count.
  • Your data is yours. Export it whenever you like. Ask for your account to be deleted and the instance and its backups go with it.

Your instance runs on a machine in Frankfurt, and the software there can read what is in it. The privacy policy has the detail.
